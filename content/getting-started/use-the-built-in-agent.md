# Use the built-in agent

BitterClip's agent sits beside the editor. Open any Episode and it is already there with a
short read of the recording and, once analysis has finished, a suggested next move. You
don't connect anything and there is no prompt to learn.

## What to ask for

Say what you want the way you'd say it to an editor:

- **A cut.** "Make a 60-second highlight of this session focused on the pricing discussion."
- **A change to the cut you have.** "Tighten the opening." "Lose the sponsor tangent."
  "Start on her answer, not the question."
- **The picture.** "Use the wide shot for the intro." "Stay on the guest through that answer."
- **Finish.** "Add a title for Jay the first time he speaks." "Put a music bed under the cold
  open." "Set the chapters."
- **Delivery.** "Render it." Then download the MP4 or copy a review link from the Outputs tab.

You don't have to phrase anything a particular way, and you don't have to know how the editor
works. Say what's wrong; the agent chooses the mechanism.

## How it works with you

- It reads the transcript and the picture before it cuts, and every cut point lands on the
  audio, never on a guess. Each change stays linked to the exact moment it came from.
- Cuts and camera changes appear as steps with **Undo**. Anything else it changes, you can
  reverse by asking.
- Nothing renders until you ask, and nothing is published without a separate "Publish now?"
  from you.
- It works on the Episode you have open. Ask for a new artifact ("make a clip of…") and it
  creates one beside the Episode instead of changing it.
