# Delete your BitterClip data

Canonical HTML page: https://bitterclip.com/data-deletion

Markdown version: https://bitterclip.com/data-deletion.md

You can remove the data BitterClip stored from a connected account—including
Facebook and Instagram—at any time. The fastest path is to disconnect the
integration in the app. You can also delete your whole account or email
hello@bitterclip.com to request deletion.

## 1. Disconnect the integration

1. Sign in at https://app.bitterclip.com and open Settings.
2. Find the connected Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, or X channel and
   choose **Disconnect**.
3. Disconnecting stops further use of that authorization and removes the stored
   access tokens and connected-account metadata for that channel.

## 2. Revoke access from the provider

For a Facebook or Instagram connection, you can also open Facebook **Settings &
privacy → Settings → Business integrations** (or **Apps and websites**), select
BitterClip, and remove it. This revokes the access BitterClip was granted,
independent of the in-app disconnect.

Facebook business integrations: https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools

## 3. Delete your whole account

To remove everything BitterClip holds for you—including connection records,
tokens, recordings, transcripts, clips, and associated workspace data—sign in
and go to https://app.bitterclip.com/account/delete.

You may instead email hello@bitterclip.com from the address on your account with
the subject **Data deletion**. We will confirm and process the request.

Some backups, logs, billing records, and security records may remain for a
limited period where required or operationally necessary, then age out.

## If you never created an account

If you reached the deletion page from a Facebook or Instagram login prompt but
never created a BitterClip account, BitterClip holds no data for you after you
remove it from Facebook business integrations. Email hello@bitterclip.com if
you would like written confirmation.

See also the BitterClip privacy policy: https://bitterclip.com/privacy
