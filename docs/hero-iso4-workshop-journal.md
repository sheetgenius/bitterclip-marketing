# ISO4 motion workshop journal

This is the working ledger for the current ISO4 hill climb. Durable product
intent lives in `docs/hero-iso4-brief.md`; this file records hypotheses,
evidence, revisions, and unresolved defects so each pass begins from observed
truth rather than taste-memory.

## Start message — 2026-08-19

Build the ingestion act as a quiet transformation of state. A real Mike & John
session comes to rest in a liquid-film threshold and slowly gives up its
information. Sparse particle triplets peel from the dissolving lower edge,
float with restraint, and are drawn into a precise writer on the lower reel.
They must visibly become new film cells, not disappear into machinery. The
last fragment completes the last thumbnail; the membrane recedes; a concealed
lamp behind the gate illuminates that thumbnail; three finished artifacts
unfold toward the viewer as one balanced holographic constellation.

Judge the motion first at 440×956. It should lower the viewer's pulse while
holding attention: mystical, inevitable, mechanically intelligible, and
beautiful across time. Keep the floating vintage dual-reel machine large and
dark, move it nearer the left edge, reveal a touch more side profile, remove
the circular front light canister, and let the work—not an exposed bulb—be the
brightest evidence.

## Evaluation harness

Every round uses the same evidence ladder:

1. Freeze deterministic frames through `window.__iso.still(t)` at the major
   causal beats, at 440×956 first and desktop second.
2. Compare adjacent frames, not only a contact-sheet endpoint. The relevant
   question is whether position, density, luminance, and attention move in one
   calm direction.
3. Inspect motion equations for endpoint velocity, acceleration continuity,
   peak speed, trail length, orbit count, and correlation with 16fps transport.
4. Run independent adversarial reviews through perception/psychology,
   motion mathematics, and cinematic VFX/composition. Reconcile repeated
   findings against owner rulings; a reviewer cannot override the brief.
5. Generate and smoke-test after each settled code pass. Browser screenshots
   are evidence of composition; build output is evidence of integrity. Neither
   substitutes for the other.

- `t=0.60`: dormant membrane and dark apparatus.

- `t=2.70`: threshold contact and first broad ripple.

## Round 1 — causal geometry and deterministic flow

Three independent reviews converged on the same diagnosis: stress came from
stacked discontinuities, not particle count alone. The old system launched all
96 particles together, expanded to full radius in 0.38s, compressed each final
pull into as little as 141ms, revolved more than once, extrapolated trails from
the prior rendered frame, and deleted particles at a bright point. The result
read as suction, insects, or destruction.

Implemented corrections:

- Kept 32 three-particle cohorts, but moved their writer onto the exposed
  descending film 0.813 world units before the lower reel's right tangent.
  The writer-to-gate loop distance is exactly 32 frame pitches, so the final
  cohort completes at `t=6.800` exactly when the first charged frame reaches
  the optical gate.
- Replaced frame-distance spirals with deterministic cubic Bezier paths and a
  quintic minimum-jerk envelope. Every path has one broad 0.55-turn curl and a
  final tangent aligned with the downward film feed.
- Assigned each cohort's source particles monotonically across three film-cell
  registration sites to prevent path crossings. Points remain at registration
  for 140ms while the real Episode 1 thumbnail resolves.
- Replaced refresh-rate-dependent trails with analytic positions sampled at a
  fixed shutter offset. Replaced hard square points with radial soft cores.
- Replaced the lower-reel target ring with a film-sized rectangular writer
  aperture and moved the real thumbnail onto the actual YZ film plane.
- Replaced the front circular canister and white orb with a shallow rectangular
  gate. The gate thumbnail is now in front of a concealed backlight.
- Shifted the mobile crop left and increased camera yaw modestly. Rebuilt the
  three outputs as a shallow triangular constellation and changed artifact
  cores from additive to normal blending so overlaps retain silhouettes.

Round 1 visual evidence showed three remaining defects: the falling file was
edge-on and unreadable; particles were too faint at phone scale; and the writer
bezel was brighter than the transformation. The descent also still crossed the
CTA band.

## Round 5 — five-state desktop adversarial review

The 1817×1454 sequence was reviewed at `t=0.6`, `3.3`, `5.8`, `8.7`, and
`11.9` by three independent internal lenses (perception, motion mathematics,
and VFX) plus AGY and Grok. All five agreed that the concept and chronology now
survive the loop; the failure is the desktop shot design.

Two implementation faults explain most of the visual damage:

- The WebGL camera is selected from canvas aspect alone. A desktop DOM can
  therefore receive an intermediate or phone-like camera with a much more
  distant position, higher look target, and different view offset. The
  discontinuity can be triggered by a very small aspect change.
- The mobile copy attenuation relied on a responsive utility that does not
  hide it in the observed desktop build. Its computed value at 909×727 is
  `display: block`, producing the large charcoal rectangle the owner saw.
  Separately, the `86vh` canvas plus bottom padding exposes a horizontal page
  band instead of one continuous noir void.

The measured final scene ends around 72% of viewport width and leaves roughly
28% unused on the right. Across the full story it grows about 125px downward
but only 29px rightward. The output therefore feels like a mobile cluster
placed in a large desktop canvas instead of a projection fan claiming the
right-hand stage.

The accepted desktop screen-space contract is:

- Copy exclusion zone: `x 18–41%`, `y 25–60%` with no bright moving geometry.
- Persistent apparatus: `x 42–59%`, `y 22–82%`, stable across all beats.
- Threshold and writer action: `x 48–70%`, `y 49–76%`.
- Output constellation: `x 56–80%`, `y 28–95%`, with the landscape deliberately
  high and the phone-to-transcript pair stepping down/right. This supersedes
  the earlier near-row band after the owner's explicit composition ruling.
- No one-pixel breakpoint jump; a 5% aspect change should move the apparatus
  centroid less than 1.5% and alter projected scale less than 3%.
- Full-frame high-luminance area remains restrained. Scale and placement, not
  extra bloom, must make the sequence legible.

The review also produced conflicting proposals. More speed, a visible ground
platen, or an already-filled threshold would undo explicit owner decisions:
the transformation must remain slow and mystical, the machine must float, and
the file must visibly become the charged film. Those proposals are rejected.
The convergent rulings are to remove the two structural seams, make camera
selection width-aware and continuous, enlarge and move the right-stage action
without moving the copy, then judge selective rim light and output separation
only after the framing is correct.
